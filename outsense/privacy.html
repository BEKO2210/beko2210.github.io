Privacy Policy — Outsense

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Outsense is an offline outdoor tool for Android. This policy describes what the app does with your data. In short: Outsense does not transmit your data to us or to any third party, and it has no technical ability to do so. App data leaves the device only in two ways, both under your control: files you export yourself, and Android's own backup — both are described below.

No internet access

Outsense does not declare the android.permission.INTERNET permission. Android therefore prevents the app from opening any network connection at all. Nothing you enter and nothing the app records is sent to us, to a server of ours, or to any third party through the app. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting service.

Data the app holds on your device

All of the following is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. The app itself never sends any of it anywhere:

  • Your last known location, altitude and barometric pressure history, used for navigation, weather and sunrise/sunset times
  • Beacons, recorded paths, notes, packing lists and tide tables you create
  • Photos you use as maps, and photos taken with the cloud scanner
  • Your step count, if you use the pedometer
  • Your settings

Location

Outsense uses your location for the compass and beacon navigation, for backtrack, for sea-level pressure calibration in the weather monitor, and to compute sunrise and sunset times. All of that happens on the device.

If you switch on sunrise and sunset alerts, the app asks for permission to use your location while it is not in use — Android calls this "Allow all the time". It needs that because the alert is produced while the app is closed and the times depend on where you actually are. The app shows you what the permission is for before asking, and you can refuse: everything else keeps working, and you can withdraw the permission at any time in Android's settings.

Backtrack and the weather monitor record while the app is in the background as well, but they do so through a visible, ongoing notification that you start and stop yourself, and they do not need the "Allow all the time" permission.

Outsense never uploads your location, never sells it, and never uses it for advertising or analytics. The only way a copy can leave the device is Android's own backup, described below, which you control.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used for the sighting compass, the camera clinometer, the cloud scanner, the QR code scanner and for photographing maps. Pictures are stored in the app's private storage. Outsense does not request microphone access.

Files you export yourself

The app can write a backup archive, GPX files of beacons and paths, and a diagnostics log. This only happens when you ask for it, and Android's own file picker lets you choose where the file goes. From that point the file is yours and this policy no longer governs it.

Android's own backup

Outsense allows Android's standard app backup, so that your beacons, paths and settings survive a new phone. Depending on your Android version, your device settings and your Google account settings, the operating system may therefore copy this app's data into your personal cloud backup.

That copy is made and controlled by Android and Google together with your own device settings — not by Outsense, and not as a data service of ours. It goes into storage that belongs to you. Outsense cannot reach into that backup itself and we never receive it; what the app can see is whatever Android restores back into its own storage on a new device. You can switch the backup off in your phone's settings, and you can delete an existing backup through your Google account.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete. To remove everything Outsense has stored, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Files you exported yourself have to be deleted where you put them, and a copy in Android's backup is removed through your Google account's backup settings.

Children

Outsense is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
