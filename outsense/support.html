Field notes / Assistance

Support

Quick answers for setup, offline use and sensor-dependent tools.

Start here

Outsense is designed to work without a network. Before leaving coverage, import or download any map files and elevation models you plan to use, and check the sensor overview inside the app.

Frequently asked questions

Why is a tool missing?

Several tools require hardware your phone may not have. The barometer, compass, step counter and light sensor are device-dependent. Outsense hides tools the device cannot support.

Does offline mean maps are included?

No. The app works without a network, but map files and elevation models must be imported or downloaded by you first.

Why does an astronomy alert ask for background location?

Sunrise and sunset alerts need your current location while the app is closed so they can calculate the correct local time. You can refuse; other features continue to work.

Does Outsense connect to the internet?

No. It does not declare android.permission.INTERNET, so Android prevents it from opening a network connection. See the privacy policy for the two named exceptions involving user exports and Android system backup.

Contact

For help, email nullmesh@protonmail.com. Include your phone model, Android version, what you expected and what happened. Do not include sensitive location data unless it is necessary.

Version and status

This support information covers Outsense 8.2.0. Outsense is not yet on Google Play; support is provided directly by email.
